Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

What this page collects

If you sign up for the relaunch notification list, the only thing collected is your email address, submitted through an embedded EmailOctopus form. That's it. This page doesn't have accounts, doesn't track what you click beyond basic anonymous visit counts, and doesn't sell or share your email with anyone.

What it's used for

Your email is used for exactly one thing: to let you know if CookCloud relaunches. It won't be used for anything else, and it won't be handed off to a third party for marketing.

Where it's stored

Signups are stored and sent through EmailOctopus, a third-party email service. Their own privacy policy governs how they handle and secure that data on their end.

Anonymous analytics

This page uses GoatCounter for basic visit counts — pageviews and referrers, nothing tied to an individual. GoatCounter doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal data, which is why there's no cookie banner on this page.

Your options

  • Every email sent to this list includes an unsubscribe link that removes you immediately.
  • You can request your email be deleted at any time, unsubscribe link or not — just reach out (see below).
  • Nothing here is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

Questions or a deletion request? Email sales@bryanheadrick.com, or reach out through bryanheadrick.com.
